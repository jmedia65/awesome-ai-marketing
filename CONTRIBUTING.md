# Contributing

Thanks for helping keep this list useful. It's curated, not a directory: most suggestions won't be added, and that's by design.

## What gets added

**Priority: open-source skills, agents and MCP servers for marketing work.** To qualify, a project needs:

- An open-source license
- Commits within the last 3 months
- Signs of real use: roughly 100+ stars, or a maintainer or organization with a track record
- Docs clear enough that a marketer can install and use it

**Official MCP servers from established vendors** (Google, HubSpot, Klaviyo and so on) are welcome.

**Commercial SaaS tools** are added rarely, at the maintainer's discretion, and only based on direct experience or strong evidence that marketers use them. Please don't open PRs for new SaaS tools. If you think one belongs here, open an issue explaining who uses it and why it beats what's already listed.

## What gets closed without review

- Undisclosed self-promotion (submitting your own product without saying so)
- Tracking or affiliate parameters in links
- Marketing copy instead of a plain description
- Tools that are mostly a UI over a single prompt

## How to submit

1. One entry per PR, in the right section.
2. Format: `- **[Name](link)**: What it does and who it's for, in one or two plain sentences. License.`
3. Mark it **vendor-backed** if the project mainly exists to lead people to a paid product.
4. Fill in the PR template, including the affiliation question.

## Removals

Entries get removed when they go unmaintained, get archived, or stop being the best option in their section. Spotted one? Open an issue or a PR. Those are always welcome.
